About

Data science with a business lens and a research spine.

I started in Business Analytics, where the question was rarely just whether a model worked. It was whether the work helped someone understand a customer, a risk, an operation, or a decision more clearly.

At Vanderbilt, I am building a deeper technical foundation in data science: machine learning, NLP, data engineering, evaluation, and research methods. I am especially interested in the space where language meets decision-making, from SEC filings and risk signals to mental health narratives and behavioral data.

The SEC Filing NLP project is my flagship AI and finance project, but it is one piece of a broader pattern. I like problems where the data is imperfect, the stakes are real, and the answer has to be explained carefully.

Flagship AI / Finance Project 01

SEC Filing NLP

Problem
Annual filings contain signals about risk, strategy, and uncertainty, but the useful language is buried in long, careful documents.
Built
An NLP pipeline for parsing filings, extracting factors, scoring sentiment, and testing whether language features add useful signal.
Outcome
A research-grade system that made dense filings easier to compare and produced a disciplined case study in evaluation and overfitting risk.
Tools
Python, NLP, transformers, sentiment scoring, backtesting, evaluation design.

Research / Social Impact 02

Mental Health NLP Research

Problem
Mental health language is subtle. The same phrase can mean different things depending on context, speaker, and timing.
Analyzed
Language patterns related to anxiety, trauma, and emotional framing, with attention to interpretability and careful claims.
Outcome
A research direction focused on using NLP as a listening tool, not a replacement for clinical judgment.
Tools
Python, text preprocessing, embeddings, topic modeling, qualitative review.
